💡 Core Principle: AI Slide Editor (SlideRev) runs 100% locally. Your PDF files, PowerPoint presentations, and images are processed entirely on your Mac and are never uploaded to any server.

1. Introduction

Thank you for choosing AI Slide Editor (SlideRev). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data. We do not collect, store, or share your documents or private files.

2. Data Collection and Processing

The core features of the application, including LaMa Background Inpainting and Vision OCR Text Recognition, run entirely using local machine learning models and system framework libraries on your device.

  • Local-Only Processing: Any PDF slides, generated PPTX presentations, or temporary images you import are kept strictly within your local app sandbox. They are never transmitted online or stored outside your device.
  • No Personal Identification: The app does not require users to register an account. We do not collect personal identifiers such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

3. Subscriptions and Payments

Our application offers an auto-renewable yearly premium subscription to unlock full features.

  • All purchase transactions and payment details are handled securely by Apple's official App Store system.
  • We use RevenueCat to manage and verify subscription receipt validation. RevenueCat only processes anonymous transaction IDs and receipt identifiers. We do not have access to your credit card details, real name, or Apple ID account credentials.
