# Product Hunt Launch: SlideRev

## Tagline
AI-Powered Slide 2 Editable PPTX – Perfectly Reconstruct Backgrounds.

## Description
Stop re-typing text from static slide images!

SlideRev is an AI-powered macOS utility that turns flattened slide images (from Midjourney, screenshots, or PDFs) back into fully functional, editable PPTX files.

Unlike traditional OCR tools, SlideRev uses on-device LaMa AI Inpainting to reconstruct the background behind the text perfectly, as if the text was never there. It then overlays the extracted text as native PPTX layers.

### Key Features:
- 🧠 **On-Device AI**: Professional-grade background removal powered by local ML models.
- 📄 **Slide 2 Editable**: Export straight to native OOXML PPTX format.
- ✨ **Batch Refinement**: Clean up entire documents with one click.
- 🔒 **Privacy First**: No cloud uploads. Your data never leaves your Mac.

## Maker's Comment
Hi Product Hunt! 👋

SlideRev was born out of a simple frustration: seeing beautiful slide designs generated by AI (like Midjourney) but being unable to edit them. You’re left with a flat image and have to spend hours re-typing text and cloning backgrounds.

We built SlideRev to "reverse" this process. By combining high-accuracy OCR with state-of-the-art LaMa Inpainting, SlideRev fills in the background where the text used to be, giving you a clean slate. Then, it reconstructs the text as editable PowerPoint layers.

It’s all 100% on-device. No cloud, no subscriptions, no privacy leaks.
