# AI Slide Editor (v1.0.0)

## 更新内容 (Release Notes)

### 简体中文

**AI Slide Editor 1.0.0 正式版发布！**

欢迎使用全新升级的 **AI Slide Editor** (原名 SlideRev)！本次更新为您带来了全面的体验升级与底层优化：

- **全新品牌升级**：为了让软件核心价值更直观，我们将应用名称正式更改为 **AI Slide Editor**，帮助您更轻松地编辑和还原任何包含文本的幻灯片图片。
- **本地化隐私优先**：进一步优化了完全本地运行的 AI 架构，无论是精准的 OCR 文本识别，还是专业的背景擦除（基于 LaMa Inpainting 模型），所有数据处理均在您的 Mac 设备端进行，100% 保护您的隐私。
- **排版还原提升**：修复了多行文本的合并与对齐问题（修复为正确保留左/中/右对齐格式，而不再强制居中），导出的原生 PPTX 文件更加完美。
- **体验优化**：提升了应用在处理复杂包含背景图像时的稳定性与运行性能。

感谢您的支持与使用！如果您觉得本工具有帮助，欢迎在 App Store 为我们留下评价。

### English

**Welcome to AI Slide Editor 1.0.0!**

We are thrilled to introduce the official 1.0.0 release of **AI Slide Editor** (formerly SlideRev), bringing you significant enhancements and a new intuitive identity.

- **New App Name:** We've rebranded from SlideRev to **AI Slide Editor** to better reflect our core mission: giving you the easiest way to edit flattened slide images using AI.
- **Privacy First, Fully Local:** Further optimized our 100% on-device AI architecture. Both high-precision OCR and professional background removal (powered by LaMa Inpainting) run entirely on your Mac. No data is ever sent to the cloud.
- **Enhanced Formatting:** Improved the paragraph grouping algorithm to accurately preserve your original text alignments (Left, Center, Right) instead of defaulting to center alignment, ensuring your exported native PPTX files are flawless.
- **Performance & Stability:** Fixed various bugs and improved the overall stability of processing high-resolution presentation images.

Thank you for your support! If you find AI Slide Editor helpful, please consider leaving us a review on the App Store.
