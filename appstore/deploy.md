## 3. Metadata (English)

*   **Promotional Text:** 
    Transform exported PDF slides back into high-fidelity, editable documents with AI-powered background erasure.

*   **Description:**
    SlideRev is a professional tool designed for academics, researchers, and office professionals who need to reverse-engineer exported PDF presentations. Using state-of-the-art AI Inpainting (LaMa), SlideRev allows you to:
    - **Erase Backgrounds:** Automatically remove watermarks, logos, or distracting backgrounds with high fidelity.
    - **Precision OCR:** Extract text with accurate positioning, color, and font style.
    - **In-place Replacement:** Replace original "burned-in" text with editable text layers in the same spot.
    - **Multi-format Export:** Save your refined slides as editable PDFs or native PPTX presentations.
    - **Privacy First:** All AI processing (Inpainting and OCR) happens 100% locally on your Mac. No data ever leaves your device.
